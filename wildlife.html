BEFORE YOU BOOK

Forest rules are part of the trip.

RouteSathi helps you choose and plan. For permits, fees, entry timing, zones and closures, the official park or forest portal is always the final authority.

Permit firstConfirm date, traveller details, gate or zone and reporting time before finalising travel.
No sighting promiseWildlife is free. A tiger or any animal sighting cannot be guaranteed by any guide or package.
Stay close enoughChoose a stay that makes early reporting comfortable instead of adding a long dark-road drive.
Respect the forestNever litter, feed animals, demand off-route driving or make noise for a photo.
