Seasonal guide

When should you visit Rajasthan?

Choose Rajasthan by weather, wildlife or festivals. This guide helps you pick the right kind of trip before you book transport and stays.

Quick rule: October to March is generally the most comfortable period for forts, old-city walks and desert travel. Summer needs early starts and a slower plan. Monsoon is greener around lakes and hills, while desert and weather-sensitive plans need flexibility.

OCTOBER – MARCH

Comfortable exploring

This is the easiest season for a first Rajasthan trip: fort mornings, markets, city walks and desert stays feel far more comfortable. It is also the busiest time around Christmas, New Year and long weekends.

JaipurForts, palaces and market walks in pleasant weather.UdaipurLake evenings, old-city lanes and relaxed sightseeing.JodhpurMehrangarh and blue-city routes without peak heat.JaisalmerThe practical season for a fort-and-desert plan.

Plan smart: Book early for Christmas, New Year, long weekends and popular desert stays.

APRIL – JUNE

Heat-smart travel

The plains can be extremely hot. Choose a hill stay or keep all outdoor stops for sunrise and late evening; avoid planning full-day fort routes in the afternoon.

Mount AbuRajasthan’s hill-station option for a cooler break.RanthamborePlan only around official safari slots and your gate.Jaipur, early-onlyUse sunrise starts, indoor breaks and late evenings.Udaipur, unhurriedLake evenings work better than midday walks.

Plan smart: Carry water, keep a shaded afternoon break and check heat alerts before travelling.

JULY – SEPTEMBER

Green & slower

Monsoon makes lake cities, stepwells and hill scenery greener. Rain can affect roads, boating and outdoor access, so keep a little buffer in your itinerary.

UdaipurLakes and Aravalli views are especially atmospheric.BundiStepwells, palace lanes and a quieter heritage pace.KotaRiver-side and garden time with weather awareness.Mount AbuA green hill escape; check rain conditions locally.

Plan smart: Verify road, boating and outdoor-site updates on the day you travel.

WILDLIFE & FESTIVALS

Choose the date carefully

A few Rajasthan experiences need the right season and advance planning more than a long city checklist.

BharatpurWinter is the key period for migratory waterfowl at Keoladeo.PushkarThe annual fair period needs early stay and transport booking.RanthamboreUse official safari availability, gate and timing information.Festival guideCheck crowd, stay and route advice before choosing dates.

Before you book

Weather, opening days, safari gates, boat rides, festival dates and road conditions can change. Check the latest official update before leaving—especially during rain, high heat and festival periods.
