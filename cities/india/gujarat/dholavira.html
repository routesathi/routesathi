YOUR READY ROUTE

Read the city.
Not the clock.

UNESCO describes Dholavira as a well-preserved Harappan urban settlement on Khadir island, with fortifications, a ceremonial ground, reservoirs and a cemetery. This route groups the heritage site in the order it makes sense on foot.

PLAN A · ONE DAY

The Dholavira heritage day

Choose this only when you have reached Dholavira the previous evening or are staying nearby. Use the morning for the site, take a genuine heat break, then visit the museum and the features you want to understand more slowly.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

For a wide Kutch itinerary, not a bigger checklist

Use a third day only when your broader Kutch trip has an independent road day. Dholavira itself does not need three packed days; the value is in avoiding an exhausting transfer before or after it.
