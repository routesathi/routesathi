PALITANA · GUJARAT

A sacred climb,
given its time.

Hundreds of Jain temples crown Shatrunjaya Hill above Palitana. The right plan begins before sunrise, keeps the summit unhurried and never treats a 3,750-step pilgrimage as a rushed day-trip add-on.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Climb early.
Keep it sacred.

Gujarat Tourism and Incredible India describe Shatrunjaya as one of Jainism’s holiest pilgrimage hills, with hundreds of temples and a climb of more than 3,750 stone steps. This route protects the energy and time that experience needs.

PLAN A · ONE DAY

The complete Shatrunjaya day

Use this only when you slept in Palitana or close by the night before. Begin early, move at your own pace, explore a focused part of the summit complex and descend with daylight and energy to spare.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

For a slower Saurashtra trip

Add a third day only when you want an independent Bhavnagar or Saurashtra road day. Palitana needs space before and after the climb—not a crowded checklist on the same afternoon.
