PORBANDAR · GUJARAT COAST

Where a quiet coast
carries a big story.

Gandhi’s birthplace, Sudama’s temple, a working sea edge and winter birdlife give Porbandar more than one reason to slow down. Start in the old city, keep the sea for evening and let the coast have its own time.

Porbandar is best when its old-city story and Arabian Sea evening get real time. Choose a focused city plan first; add birding or Madhavpur only when you have another half or full day.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Start with the story.
End by the sea.

Gujarat Tourism identifies Porbandar with Gandhi’s birthplace, Sudama heritage, beaches and birdwatching. This guide keeps those experiences in one sensible sequence instead of sending you back and forth across the coast.

PLAN A · ONE DAY

Porbandar’s essential city day

Begin with the Gandhi heritage core while your mind is fresh, keep Sudama Mandir close to the same old-city block, then slow down rather than forcing a distant beach before sunset.
