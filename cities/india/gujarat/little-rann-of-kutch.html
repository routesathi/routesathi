LITTLE RANN OF KUTCH · GUJARAT

Wide salt land,
wild on its own terms.

Indian wild ass, winter birds and a huge salt-desert horizon make Little Rann unlike the White Rann. Give the sanctuary an early safari, a close stay and enough time to let wildlife set the day’s pace.

SEASON + CLOSURE

Winter is strongest

Gujarat Tourism lists winter, November–February, as the best birding period and currently posts sanctuary access from 16 October to 15 June. Treat this as a check point, not a permanent promise—confirm before booking.
