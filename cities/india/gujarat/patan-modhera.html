PATAN + MODHERA · GUJARAT

Stone, silk
and sunlight.

Start with Rani ki Vav while the air is cooler, keep Patan’s heritage together, then drive once to Modhera for the Sun Temple. It is one proper heritage day—not three separate detours.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Stepwell first.
Sun Temple later.

Gujarat Tourism's suggested route also keeps Patan before Modhera. It lists the Patan–Modhera drive at about 45 km / one hour, so this order avoids repeated road time.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Patan–Modhera heritage circuit

Best first-time plan from Ahmedabad, Mehsana or a Patan stay. Use a car/cab for the single Patan-to-Modhera transfer and keep the heritage visits unhurried.

1 full day Best first visit

MORNING · PATAN

  1. 08:30 Rani ki VavBegin at the UNESCO stepwell while it is cooler. Explore the levels and carvings slowly; Gujarat Tourism estimates about two hours.
  2. 11:00 Patola heritage / old PatanChoose a Patola-related visit only if it is open and you have confirmed access. Keep it in the same town-side block.
  3. 13:00 Lunch in PatanEat before the Modhera drive. Do not leave city-side food to chance during the monument transfer.

AFTERNOON · MODHERA

  1. 14:30 Drive Patan to ModheraPlan roughly an hour plus local buffer. This is the only main transfer in the route.
  2. 15:45 Modhera Sun TempleExplore the Surya Kund, Sabha Mandap and temple complex in softer afternoon light. It is an ASI-protected monument; follow current entry rules.
  3. 18:00 Return or stay nearbyLeave with daylight/road buffer. Keep evening events separate unless you have confirmed the current schedule.

These are optional additions, not things to force into the main circuit.

TEXTILE HERITAGE

Patan Patola

Patan is known for double-ikat Patola weaving. Confirm a museum/workshop visit before building your schedule around it.

EVENING OPTION

Rani ki Vav programme

Gujarat Tourism lists evening projection-mapping slots, but check the current time and operation before changing your travel plan.

EXTRA HERITAGE DAY

Sidhpur

Use this only with another day. It does not belong between Patan and Modhera on a tight circuit.

FESTIVAL NOTE

Modhera Dance Festival

The annual festival is generally around the weekend after Uttarayan. Treat dates and access as live information, not a fixed promise.

USE THIS BEFORE LEAVING

Small checks.
Big difference.

Start with Rani ki Vav

The stepwell is the longest exploration stop. Start early and do not keep it for a rushed final hour.

Official Rani ki Vav information ↗

Keep one vehicle

A cab/car is simplest for Patan–Modhera. Do not depend on multiple local changes if you have a same-day return.

Check Modhera access

Modhera is ASI-protected. Confirm live entry/ticket timing and any event schedule before the trip.

Official Modhera information ↗

What to skip if short

Skip Patola or Sidhpur before cutting Rani ki Vav or Modhera. The two monuments are the real route.

Timings and programmes can change. This plan follows Gujarat Tourism and ASI guidance; confirm tickets, access and transport before travelling.
