How much time do you have?

Somnath is a pilgrimage-first destination. A good plan leaves time for security, darshan and stillness; it is not improved by adding a safari or every nearby attraction in the same day.

USE THIS BEFORE LEAVING

Small checks.
Big difference.

Leave electronics safely

The Somnath Trust says mobiles, cameras, smartwatches and other electronic equipment are prohibited inside. Use the free cloakroom/lockers and do not plan on using your phone during darshan.

Check official visitor rules ↗

Confirm aarti and show timing

Schedules can change for weather, monsoon, festivals and local arrangements. Check the Trust’s current details on the day before making an evening reservation.

Use a realistic local ride

Triveni is close to the temple, while Bhalka is a separate town-side stop. One direct auto/taxi sequence is simpler than booking multiple point-to-point rides.

What to skip when time is short

With less than a full day, choose Somnath Temple and either Triveni or Bhalka. Do not put Gir safari, Diu or a long road trip into the same visit.

Timings, rules and access can change. This plan uses the Shree Somnath Trust, Gujarat Tourism and Gir Somnath district guidance. Recheck darshan, electronics policy, transport and weather before travelling.
