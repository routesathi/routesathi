PICK THE RIGHT WINDOW

One state,
different seasons.

Use these as planning rules, not fixed promises. Every guide still asks you to check live local access before travelling.

OCTOBER TO FEBRUARY

Best all-round Gujarat window

Comfortable for heritage walks, temple coasts, longer drives and city guides.

Most tripsCooler days

BEST FOR

  1. HeritageAhmedabad, Vadodara, Patan–Modhera and JunagadhUse the cooler mornings for forts, stepwells and old-city lanes.
  2. Temple coastDwarka and SomnathPlan sunrise/temple blocks with a sea-side evening buffer.

CHECK FIRST

  1. White RannDhordo / Rann events and permitsBook only after checking the current stay, permit and festival arrangements.
  2. GirOfficial safari availabilityUse the official permit system; weekend and festival demand can change quickly.

MARCH TO JUNE

Start early, keep days lighter

Heat changes what feels sensible. Choose one strong morning, a slower afternoon and an evening plan—not a long all-day checklist.

Early startsHeat-aware plan

WORKS WELL

  1. MorningFort, temple or heritage blockFinish outdoor sightseeing before the hottest hours.
  2. EveningRiverfront, lakefront or food laneKeep the final stop close to your stay.

AVOID

  1. No over-packed drivesDo not combine distant monumentsSummer is not the season for zig-zag routes.
  2. GirnarOnly with realistic fitness and live conditionsNever treat a mountain day as a casual afternoon stop.

JULY TO SEPTEMBER

Green, dramatic—and variable

Monsoon can be beautiful around hills and lakes, but road, outdoor and wildlife access needs a live check.

Flexible planWeather-led

GOOD CHOICES

  1. City staysAhmedabad or VadodaraKeep indoor heritage, food and museum options as a weather backup.
  2. Junagadh sideOnly with local weather awarenessEnjoy the landscape, but do not rely on a fixed mountain schedule.

DO NOT ASSUME

  1. Wildlife safariGir closure and permit rulesCheck the official portal before making any wildlife plan.
  2. Desert conditionsRann accessDo not use winter itineraries as monsoon itineraries.
