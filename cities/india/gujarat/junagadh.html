PLAN A · ONE DAY

Junagadh essentials

Uparkot, Ashokan history and Mahabat Maqbara—best for a first city visit.

  1. 08:00 · Uparkot FortStart with the fort, wells and nearby historic remains while the day is cooler.
  2. 11:30 · Ashokan Rock Edicts areaKeep this with the Uparkot side rather than crossing the city twice.
  3. 15:30 · Mahabat MaqbaraSee the exterior and surrounding old-city architecture in softer afternoon light.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

City + Girnar, properly

The balanced plan: history first, then an unhurried mountain and temple day.

  1. Day 1 · City heritageFollow the one-day route without forcing Girnar into the evening.
  2. Day 2 · Girnar early startChoose the ropeway or your own stair-and-temple plan; upper temples need much more time.
  3. Late day · Return calmlyKeep the final hours flexible for rest, meals or a city stroll.

PRACTICAL NOTES

Plan the difficult part first.

Stay: City-centre accommodation works best for the heritage day; stay near Taleti only if Girnar is your priority.

Transport: Junagadh is connected by road and rail; Gujarat Tourism lists Rajkot, Keshod and Porbandar as nearby airports.

Wildlife: Gir/Sasan is a separate wildlife trip. Do not promise yourself a safari after a full Girnar day; permits must use the official booking system.
