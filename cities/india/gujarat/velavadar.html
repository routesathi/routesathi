YOUR READY ROUTE

Follow the grassland.
Not a wildlife promise.

Gujarat Tourism describes Velavadar as a 34 sq km grassland national park north of Bhavnagar, known for blackbuck, nilgai and wintering harriers. This route gives its early field time the respect it needs.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

For patient winter birding

A third day makes sense for birders and slow nature travellers, particularly in the winter birding window. It is not a reason to repeat long drives or demand rare sightings.
