Royal rooms,
Kutch stories.

Bhuj works best in two clear blocks: its palace-and-lake heritage in the city core, then Bhujodi crafts and Smritivan with real travel time. The White Rann is a different journey altogether.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Old Bhuj first.
Crafts later.

Aina Mahal and Prag Mahal are in the Darbargadh heritage complex. Keep that whole side together, then use another block for Bhujodi and Smritivan rather than chasing the White Rann.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Bhuj heritage core

Best first visit with limited time: palaces, museum history and the lake side in one compact route.

1 full dayBest first visit

MORNING · DARBARGADH SIDE

  1. 09:00Aina MahalBegin with the palace’s mirror-work and Kutch collection; verify current entry hours before leaving.
  2. 10:45Prag MahalKeep this Italian-Gothic palace in the same heritage block—it is beside Aina Mahal.
  3. 13:00Lunch near the city coreTake a proper break before the afternoon museum-and-lake side.

AFTERNOON · LAKE SIDE

  1. 15:00Kutch MuseumUse it as the deeper Kutch-history stop if the day’s opening schedule suits you.
  2. 16:45Hamirsar LakeFinish with a slower lakeside walk close to the old-city sights.
  3. 18:30Stay in BhujDo not begin a White Rann drive now; keep the evening local or prepare for the next day.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

Heritage + crafts and Smritivan

The balanced plan: complete the old-city route first, then give Kutch crafts and the earthquake memorial their own unhurried day.

2 daysBalanced Bhuj

DAY 1 · OLD BHUJ

  1. MorningAina Mahal + Prag MahalFollow the heritage-core route above without adding a long outside transfer.
  2. AfternoonKutch Museum + HamirsarKeep the museum and lake side together, with time to slow down.

DAY 2 · CRAFT + MEMORIAL

  1. MorningBhujodiVisit the weaving and craft village with time to see work respectfully, not just shop quickly.
  2. AfternoonSmritivanUse the Earthquake Memorial Museum as a separate reflective visit; check its museum day and entry details.
  3. EveningBhuj returnKeep onward transport relaxed, or start a separate Rann of Kutch overnight only the next morning.
