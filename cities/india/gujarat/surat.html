  1. 09:00 Surat CastleStart at the restored fort on the Tapi side; keep time for its history and galleries.
  2. 11:30 Dutch / English cemetery areaChoose one confirmed heritage stop and keep it in the same old-city block.
  3. 13:30 Lunch in townEat before the only main transfer of the day.

EVENING · DUMAS SIDE

  1. 16:30 Drive to Dumas BeachLeave only after the city-route is complete; traffic buffer matters.
  2. 17:30 Dumas BeachUse the coast for a walk and sunset rather than a rushed daytime check-in.
  3. 19:30 Return / dinnerKeep the final meal close to your stay or the return route.
