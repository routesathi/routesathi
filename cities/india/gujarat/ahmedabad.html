FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much time do you have?

Ahmedabad is India’s first UNESCO World Heritage City. Its best experience is not a long place-list: begin with the pols and old-city monuments, then choose the river side or Adalaj based on your actual time.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Old lanes first.
River after heat.

The heritage core is compact but crowded; it works best in the morning. Sabarmati Ashram and the Riverfront are on the western side, so keep them after lunch rather than travelling back into the old city.

OFFICIAL HERITAGE WALK

Kalupur Swaminarayan Temple → the pols

Gujarat Tourism lists AMC’s daily Heritage Walk from Kalupur Swaminarayan Temple, ending at Jama Masjid around 10:30 AM. Confirm the walk arrangement on the day; if you go independently, do not enter private homes or religious spaces without permission.

OLD CITY · WALKABLE

Pol lanes, chabutras and Manek Chowk

Slow down for the carved wooden houses and neighbourhood details. This is a living residential area, not an open-air museum—keep noise low and avoid blocking lanes.

USE THIS BEFORE LEAVING

Small checks.
Big difference.

Confirm the Heritage Walk

Gujarat Tourism lists it as a daily 7:45 AM route from Kalupur Temple to Jama Masjid. Check the current arrangement before leaving your hotel.

Check official listing ↗

Religious-site access first

At temples and mosques, prayer time, dress, footwear and photography rules always come before your itinerary.

Do not assume Calico entry

Only go when your advance reservation is confirmed. Use the day for Adalaj or Kankaria if it is not.

What to skip if short on time

Do not combine Old City, Sabarmati, Adalaj and Kankaria in one day. Pick the first two—your experience will be better.

Timings and visitor rules change. This route uses Gujarat Tourism and UNESCO guidance. Confirm religious-site access, museum bookings, weather and transport before travelling.
