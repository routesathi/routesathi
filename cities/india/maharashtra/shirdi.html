01 · OFFICIAL PORTAL

Use Sai Sansthan only

Book or check availability through the official online Sai Sansthan portal; do not send money or OTPs to an agent.

02 · ID + ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

Match every traveller

The booked person and original photo ID matter. Read your acknowledgement and report to the official point at the stated time.

03 · STAY AFTER PASS

Protect the temple slot

Choose a stay that lets you reach the complex early and calmly. Do not book a distant hotel if an early reporting time is already confirmed.

Simple rule:Official pass first, stay second, travel ticket third. A calm Shirdi visit is more valuable than stacking Shani Shingnapur, a long road transfer and a booked darshan on one day.
