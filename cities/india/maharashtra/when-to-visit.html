MAHARASHTRA · SEASONAL GUIDE

Choose the season,
then the route.

Maharashtra has very different travel conditions across hill stations, caves, cities, coast and wildlife. Start with the guide that is already live; new seasonal picks will be added only with researched routes.

RouteSathi rule: Rain, road visibility, slippery paths, crowd levels and site access can change quickly in the Sahyadris. A good Maharashtra trip uses the current forecast and does not force every viewpoint into one day.
OCTOBER – FEBRUARY

Clearer hill days and heritage time

This is generally the easier period for outdoor cave visits, walks and viewpoints around Lonavala–Khandala. Verify current access and transport before leaving.

Lonavala–KhandalaKarla/Bhaja heritage and hill viewpoints with a more comfortable walking window.

Plan smart: Keep the caves and a viewpoint in one realistic route; Pawna and Lohagad deserve their own time.

JUNE – SEPTEMBER

Green, dramatic and weather-led

Maharashtra Tourism highlights Lonavala–Khandala as a major monsoon hill getaway. Rain can also make roads slippery and reduce visibility, so flexible timing matters more than a long checklist.

Lonavala–KhandalaUse rain gear, a current weather check and a shorter, safer plan.

Plan smart: Do not rely on a waterfall, fort path or viewpoint being safe or clearly visible in heavy rain.

MARCH – MAY

Early starts and lighter plans

Hill weather can still be a break from city heat, but walking and long exposed viewpoints feel better early in the day. Carry water and avoid turning a hot afternoon into a long outdoor loop.

Lonavala–KhandalaStart at heritage sites early and keep your afternoon plan short and shaded.

Before you book

Weather, road conditions, cave access, local transport, viewpoint restrictions and ticket rules can change. Use official information and on-site instructions as the final authority before you travel.
