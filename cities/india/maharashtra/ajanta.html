Ajanta is closed on Mondays according to Maharashtra Tourism. Entry arrangement, shuttle/parking flow, cave access, photography rules, rain conditions and food availability can change. Check current official information before leaving, respect paintings and do not use flash where prohibited.

YOUR READY AJANTA PLAN

Paintings first.
No cave-count race.

Maharashtra Tourism describes Ajanta’s thirty Buddhist caves in a horseshoe-shaped Waghora River ravine. This route uses a full daylight block for the complex, with enough breaks to actually see the art.

PLAN C · BEFORE YOU BOOK

Protect the caves, protect your day.

The art at Ajanta is sensitive and the site’s practical rules matter. Decide your transport and stay only after checking the current open day, road conditions and whether your group can walk at an unhurried pace.

4 checksBefore travel day
01 · MONDAY CLOSURE

Check the open day

Maharashtra Tourism lists Ajanta as closed on Mondays. Confirm any holiday or special arrangement directly before finalising transport.

02 · CONSERVATION RULES

Follow every sign

Painted caves face visitor-pressure and conservation needs. Respect flash, touch, noise and access restrictions without argument.

03 · TRANSPORT BUFFER

Do not book a tight return

The nearest large city/rail options require real road time. Keep your return flexible rather than cutting the cave visit short.

Simple rule:Ajanta needs one full day. Ellora, Daulatabad, Bibi Ka Maqbara and city sightseeing all belong to separate time blocks.
