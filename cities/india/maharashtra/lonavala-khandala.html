FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much time do you really have?

A focused day is enough for Karla Caves and a hill-view evening. A second day lets you choose Pawna Lake or Lohagad Fort properly. The route becomes worse—not better—when both are crammed into a short weekend stop.
