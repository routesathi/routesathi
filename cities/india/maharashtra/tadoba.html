FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How many Tadoba nights do you really have?

Tadoba is not a same-day attraction. Reach your gate-side stay the day before a morning safari. Two nights give two confirmed safari windows; three nights give a calmer forest-focused break.

YOUR READY SAFARI PLAN

Arrive early.
Let the forest set the pace.

Maharashtra Tourism identifies Tadoba–Andhari as a major tiger reserve, and the official Tadoba portal warns travellers to use only authorised safari booking. This guide keeps gate, permit and rest time ahead of rushed sightseeing.

PLAN A · TWO NIGHTS

The right first Tadoba trip

One full day gives two safari windows when both are confirmed. The exact reporting and safari time comes from the live permit—do not use a fixed old screenshot or a reseller’s message.

PLAN C · BEFORE YOU PAY

Permit first. Gate-side stay second.

Tadoba safari planning is not a package-shopping race. First get the actual official permit and details; then choose a stay close enough for a calm early report.

4 checksBefore booking stay
01 · OFFICIAL PORTAL

Book your safari safely

Use the government-authorised MyTadoba website; avoid sites promising direct gypsy or guaranteed sightings.

02 · PERMIT DETAILS

Read every field

Confirm the date, session, gate, guest names and current identification requirements before paying for travel.

03 · STAY MATCH

Choose the correct base

Book the room after you know the reporting side. Gates are not automatically quick transfers from each other.

Ethical safari:Never ask for animal feeding, loud calls, off-route driving or a “guaranteed tiger” arrangement. The forest and current official rules decide the experience.
