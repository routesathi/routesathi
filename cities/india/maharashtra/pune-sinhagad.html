YOUR READY ROUTE

Old Pune first.
Sinhagad next.

Maharashtra Tourism lists Shaniwar Wada, Aga Khan Palace, Raja Dinkar Kelkar Museum and Sinhagad among Pune’s key heritage attractions. This guide puts the close old-city sites first, then keeps the hill fort on its own day.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

Old Pune first, Sinhagad and Khadakwasla next

Day 1 follows the heritage route. Day 2 starts early for Sinhagad, with its road and fort walking included; Khadakwasla is an optional calm finish on the return rather than another must-do race.

Sinhagad involves a hill road, exposed walking and weather changes. Keep it early, check current conditions and do not promise a fast return on weekends or in monsoon.
