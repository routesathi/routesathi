PLAN C · BEFORE YOU BOOK

Check coast conditions before the hotel

Alibaug is easier when you let live tide, ferry and weather information decide the exact order. A hotel booking does not make a fort crossing, boat or water activity safe.

4 checksBefore travel day
01 · FORT ACCESS

Check the tide window

Kolaba Fort access is tide-dependent. Check current local conditions on the day and do not treat an old blog time as final.

02 · SEA TRAVEL

Confirm ferry live

If using a Mumbai–Mandwa ferry connection, confirm its current operator schedule and weather status before booking a tight return.

03 · COASTAL SAFETY

Keep water plans flexible

Monsoon, currents, beach conditions and local permissions can change. Use only a current, licensed local operator where applicable.

Simple rule:One fort window, one beach side and one comfortable return plan make a better Alibaug trip than trying to tick off every beach around Raigad.
