NASHIK + TRIMBAKESHWAR · MAHARASHTRA

Faith, caves and vines,
without the zig-zag.

Panchavati is a walkable old-city morning. Pandavleni is its own hillside stop. Trimbakeshwar needs real queue time—so a good Nashik trip gives each direction the time it deserves.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

Old Nashik first, Trimbakeshwar next

Day 1 follows the city route. Day 2 is an early Trimbakeshwar direction with an intentionally flexible temple block; a vineyard visit is only an adult optional stop after the return, never part of the darshan schedule.
