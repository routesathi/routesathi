FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much heritage time do you really have?

One day is a city-only introduction. Two days is the balanced first visit: city heritage first, then Ellora and Ghrishneshwar. A third day can be Daulatabad; Ajanta needs one extra full day beyond this guide.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

City heritage first, Ellora and Ghrishneshwar next

Day 1 follows the city route. Day 2 is deliberately a single north-west heritage direction: Ellora first, then Ghrishneshwar. Daulatabad is saved for a third day so Ellora is not reduced to a rushed stop.

Add one direction, not every remaining pin
