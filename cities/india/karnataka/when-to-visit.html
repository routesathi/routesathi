KARNATAKA · SEASONAL GUIDE

Choose the season,
then the route.

Karnataka changes between warm heritage cities, monsoon hill roads and peak-festival crowds. Start with a route already live; use current weather, event and access information before setting out.

RouteSathi rule: Mysuru’s Palace, Chamundi Hill and Brindavan Gardens have different crowd and timing patterns. In Dasara or rain, use a shorter backup plan instead of forcing every stop into the same day.
OCTOBER – FEBRUARY

Comfortable heritage walks and clearer city days

This is generally the easier window for central Mysuru walks, Palace time and Chamundi Hill. Dasara commonly falls around October and makes Mysuru especially festive—but it also brings large crowds, traffic changes and price pressure.

MysuruUse the Palace, Devaraja Market and one city museum on one day; keep the hill and KRS side separate.Brindavan GardensPlan the garden for a separate evening after checking current entry and fountain arrangements.

Plan smart: During Dasara, book transport and stays early, expect security or route changes and treat current local notices as final.

JUNE – SEPTEMBER

Green weather, but keep a rain backup

Mysuru can still be rewarding in rain, but outdoor visibility, hill roads and garden time become weather dependent. Keep indoor Palace or art-gallery time as a backup rather than promising a long exposed route.

Mysuru city coreUse Palace and a museum/market block; decide the evening only after weather and local conditions are clear.Before you leaveCheck rain, road conditions, hill access and garden programme details for the actual day.

Plan smart: Do not rely on every viewpoint, garden show or hill road being comfortably accessible in heavy rain.

MARCH – MAY

Early starts and slower afternoons

Use the cooler morning for Chamundi Hill or the Palace, then take a proper lunch break and choose an indoor or shaded afternoon stop. Avoid turning the hottest part of the day into a long outdoor walk.

One-day MysuruBegin at Palace opening, keep the market short and use an indoor heritage choice after lunch.Two-day MysuruKeep Chamundi early and shift Brindavan Gardens to the later, cooler part of the second day.

Before you book

Weather, monument entry, Palace illumination, temple queues, garden programmes, road conditions and local transport can change. Use official information and on-site instructions as the final authority before you travel.
