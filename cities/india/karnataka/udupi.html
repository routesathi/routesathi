FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much coast time do you really have?

One day covers Udupi’s temple-town side plus a Malpe sunset. Two days give the balanced coast plan if the ferry runs. A third day makes Kaup feel relaxed instead of becoming one more beach ticked off a list.

YOUR READY COASTAL PLAN

Temple first.
Sea when it is safe.

Karnataka Tourism lists Udupi’s Sri Krishna Temple, Malpe Beach, St. Mary’s Island and Kaup among the area’s key experiences. The route works when temple, ferry and coast have separate jobs—and the sea gets the final say.

Udupi temple town, local food and Malpe sunset

Use the morning for Sri Krishna Temple and the immediate town side. Keep Malpe for later light, so there is no pressure to catch an island ferry on a one-day visit.

MALPE BEACH + SEA WALK

PLAN B · TWO FULL DAYS

Temple town first, then Malpe and St. Mary’s Island

Day 1 follows the Udupi temple-town plan above. Day 2 begins only after the Malpe ferry is confirmed. Karnataka Tourism says St. Mary’s Island is accessed exclusively by ferry and seasonal sea conditions decide operation.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Use the third day for Kaup’s separate coast.

After the temple-town day and the ferry-dependent island day, Kaup Beach gives a calm final afternoon. Karnataka Tourism recommends early mornings or late afternoons there; treat lighthouse entry as a bonus only when open.
