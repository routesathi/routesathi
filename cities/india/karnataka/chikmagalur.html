FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How many hill days do you really have?

One day is a gentle coffee-town introduction. Two days are the balanced Chikmagalur plan: town side first, then Mullayanagiri. A third day unlocks Baba Budangiri without turning the hills into a race.

YOUR READY HILL PLAN

Town side first.
High hills next.

Chikmagalur is better when each side has a purpose. Karnataka Tourism places Mullayanagiri and Baba Budangiri among the region’s major hill experiences; the sensible route is to give each its own daylight block, not to stack them together.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Coffee country, Chikmagalur town and Hirekolale Lake

This is the easy introduction day. Estate visits are not public by default, so book directly with a responsible host before you build the day around one.

HIREKOLALE LAKE

Use the later light for a calm water-and-hills stop

Hirekolale Lake is useful as a low-effort scenic evening from Chikmagalur. Keep plenty of daylight for the return and do not stand on unsafe lake edges or drive fast on hill roads after rain.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Give Baba Budangiri and Manikyadhara their own hill day.

Use this after the coffee-town side and Mullayanagiri. Baba Budangiri is around 30 km from Chikmagalur town according to Karnataka Tourism, so treat it as a separate circuit and check weather before setting out.
