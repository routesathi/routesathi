YOUR READY WILDLIFE PLAN

Gate first.
Forest later.

Karnataka Tourism identifies Kabini on the Nagarhole landscape as a Forest Department safari experience, with jeep and boat formats subject to availability. This plan starts from the permit and reporting point—not from an unrealistic sightseeing checklist.

Only when your safari slot is already confirmed

This is a short wildlife break, not a guaranteed animal-viewing trip. Arrive well before the reporting point, check in, follow the actual permit instructions and stay close after the safari.

STAY / MEAL / REST

PLAN B · TWO NIGHTS

Two safari chances, with real forest rest in between

Use Day 1 for arrival and a confirmed afternoon/evening slot if available. Day 2 uses a confirmed morning safari, then a long rest, with a second slot only if you have booked it. Day 3 is departure after breakfast.

2 chancesBest balanced first trip
