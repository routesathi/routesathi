FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much Mysuru time do you really have?

One day is enough for royal heritage in the city core. Two days is the balanced first trip: a calm hill morning plus the KRS/Brindavan evening. Add Srirangapatna only with a third day.

YOUR READY MYSURU PLAN

Palace first.
Garden later.

Karnataka Tourism and the Mysuru district guide highlight the Palace, Chamundi Hills, city museums and Brindavan Gardens. This route puts each cluster into a realistic part of the day instead of treating the city as a random pin list.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Royal heritage and a city evening

Begin when Mysuru Palace opens, walk the nearby market zone, then use the afternoon for one museum or church stop. Finish with a simple local evening—do not try to add Chamundi Hill or Brindavan Gardens.

1 city areaComfortable first visit
Mysuru Palace→Devaraja Market→Lunch→Jaganmohan Palace→St. Philomena’s

The district guide lists Mysuru Palace’s visitor hours as 10:00 AM–5:30 PM. Reach near opening, allow time for the interiors and use the current ticket and camera instructions at the gate.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

A real Mysuru second day

Use Day 1 for the central heritage plan. Day 2 begins at Chamundi Hill while the light is cooler, then uses the later half for Brindavan Gardens and KRS—two locations that make sense together.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Add Srirangapatna, not a longer checklist.

Only after the city core and hill/garden day are complete, give Srirangapatna its own heritage day. It is on the Bengaluru side, so it should not be squeezed into a Mysuru city evening.
