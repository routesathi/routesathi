Rafting flow, forest permits, safari slots, weather, river level and Syntheri Rocks access can change. Book only verified/authorised operators, wear every safety item issued, carry a change of clothes and never expect a wildlife sighting. Forest and river staff instructions are final.

FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How many forest days do you really have?

One day means choose either rafting or a forest-side route. Two days are the balanced Dandeli plan: river day first, safari and Syntheri Rocks next. A third day allows one smaller nature activity without overbooking the forest.

YOUR READY NATURE PLAN

River first.
Forest next.

Karnataka Tourism places rafting on the Kali River, forest safari, Syntheri Rocks and cave/river activities among Dandeli’s key experiences. The practical route separates water activity from forest logistics and leaves the operator or reserve in control of safety decisions.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Choose one: confirmed rafting or a quiet forest-side visit

With one day, do not book a rafting slot and also promise yourself a full sanctuary circuit. Choose the experience that is confirmed for your date, and leave time for transport, briefing and weather changes.

PLAN B · TWO FULL DAYS

Rafting on Day 1, safari and Syntheri Rocks on Day 2

Day 1 follows the confirmed river-activity plan above. Day 2 starts with the official safari only if booked, then uses the forest side for Syntheri Rocks with daylight and access buffer. One safari session is enough—there is no animal-sighting promise.
