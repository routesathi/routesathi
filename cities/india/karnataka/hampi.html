FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much Hampi time do you really have?

One day gives you the sacred centre only. Two days is the balanced first visit: sacred Hampi, then Vittala and the royal enclosure. Add Anegundi or a slower monument day only with a third day.

YOUR READY HAMPI PLAN

One zone.
One real day.

UNESCO describes Hampi as a vast Vijayanagara capital with sacred, royal and urban remains across a large landscape. Karnataka Tourism recommends two to three days; this route follows that scale instead of pretending every monument belongs in one loop.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Hampi’s sacred centre, at a human pace

Use the day for the central temple and boulder landscape only. Walk the core in a simple direction, take a true midday break and keep the afternoon monuments nearby.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

Vittala first, royal Hampi later

Use Day 1 for the sacred-centre route above. On Day 2, dedicate the early hours to Vittala Temple and Stone Chariot, then move in one direction toward the royal zone after a proper meal break.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Add Anegundi side, not another rushed loop.

Only after Hampi’s sacred and royal zones are complete, choose the Anegundi/Sanapur side as a separate day. River crossing, road and local transport arrangements can change, so set the final route after checking current conditions.
