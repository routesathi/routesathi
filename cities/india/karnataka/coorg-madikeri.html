FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much Coorg time do you really have?

One day is a focused Madikeri plan. Two days is the balanced first trip: town-side heritage and the Kushalnagar/Dubare side. A third day gives Talacauvery–Bhagamandala enough road time.

YOUR READY COORG PLAN

Town first.
Valley later.

Karnataka Tourism groups Madikeri, Abbey Falls, Dubare, the Golden Temple and Talacauvery among Coorg’s key experiences. This plan respects the district’s hill-road distances rather than making a long, random sightseeing loop.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Madikeri town, Abbey Falls and a valley sunset

Start at Abbey Falls when it opens, return through Madikeri’s close heritage stops, and finish at Raja’s Seat. This makes one short out-and-back from town—not three different sides of Coorg.

1 town zoneComfortable first visit
Abbey Falls→Madikeri Fort→Omkareshwara→Lunch→Raja’s Seat
09:00–10:20 · 75–80 MINUTES

ABBEY FALLS

Begin with the waterfall before the crowds build

Karnataka Tourism lists Abbey Falls as open 9:00 AM–5:00 PM and reachable through a coffee-plantation approach. Use the official viewing area, allow time for the steps and do not enter the water.

MADIKERI FORT

Return to town for the heritage block

Use the fort as a town-centre stop after Abbey Falls. Check current visitor access before relying on interiors; if access changes, keep the route local rather than driving to a distant substitute.

OMKARESHWARA TEMPLE

Keep one nearby temple stop

Omkareshwara Temple is close to the Fort and belongs in the same town walk. Follow current worship and photography rules; it is a living temple, not just a photo stop.

RAJA’S SEAT

Finish with the Madikeri sunset side

Karnataka Tourism lists Raja’s Seat as open 9:00 AM–8:00 PM. Use the late-afternoon view only if weather is clear enough; mist can be beautiful, but it can also remove the long valley view.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

Madikeri first, Kushalnagar side next

Use Day 1 for the Madikeri route above. Day 2 moves in one direction through the Kushalnagar/Dubare side: check responsible elephant-experience arrangements first, then keep Nisargadhama and the Golden Temple in the same wider zone.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Bhagamandala and Talacauvery get a real day.

Talacauvery is not a quick Madikeri add-on. Use the third day to travel through Bhagamandala, then up to Talacauvery with weather and road conditions setting the pace.
