MAIN VISIT · GOLDEN FORT

Jaisalmer Fort (Sonar Qila)

Start early and walk slowly. This is a living fort with homes, shops, hotels and temples inside—not a closed monument complex. Respect residents and keep lanes clear.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

Golden City + one desert evening

This is the RouteSathi recommendation for a short visit. Day 1 is fully inside Jaisalmer. Day 2 starts light, then moves once toward Sam for sunset, dinner and your return or camp stay.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Desert night, without compromising the city

Use the first two days as above. The third day lets you enjoy a desert sunrise and return calmly instead of forcing a long road journey immediately after the camp.

3 daysBest relaxed plan
DAY 1

Golden City core

Fort, Jain Temples, Patwon Ki Haveli and Gadisar Lake—fully city based.

DAY 2

One light city add-on + Sam

Choose one extra haveli or Kuldhara route, then reach Sam well before sunset and stay overnight.

DAY 3

Sunrise & return

Enjoy sunrise only if it suits you, have breakfast, check out and return to Jaisalmer before your train/road journey. Keep a buffer.

Why 3 days is best:The desert camp becomes an experience, not a rushed evening between fort sightseeing and an overnight journey.
