FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How many days do you have?

Bundi is compact but layered. Rajasthan Tourism lists Garh Palace, Taragarh, Raniji ki Baori, Dabhai Kund, Sukh Mahal and Jait Sagar among the key places—so do not turn every stop into a quick photo break.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Start in town.
Climb only once.

Garh Palace is listed by Rajasthan Tourism as open from 8 AM to 5 PM, but entry and access can change. Confirm that day’s timing before you leave your stay.

PLAN A · ONE DAY

The essential Bundi heritage route

Best for a first visit with one full day. Begin with the palace before it gets busy, keep nearby stepwells together, then choose Taragarh only if access, weather and your energy are right.

MAIN STOP · PALACE

Garh Palace + Chitrashala

Give Bundi’s palace murals and courtyards proper time. Rajasthan Tourism highlights Chhatra Mahal, Phool Mahal, Badal Mahal and the Chitrashala gallery—use a guide if you want detailed painting context.

OLD TOWN · WATER HERITAGE

Dabhai Kund

This inverted-pyramid stepwell is close to the palace zone. Keep it as a short architectural stop; follow barriers and do not climb into closed sections for photos.

Have lunch in town and refill water. The fort climb and uneven surfaces are more enjoyable when you are not doing them in the hottest part of the day.

SIGNATURE STEP-WELL

Raniji ki Baori

Rajasthan Tourism describes the Queen’s Stepwell as a 1699, multi-storeyed baori with carved pillars and a high arched entrance. Allow time for the architecture; entry conditions can vary.

Open Raniji ki Baori in Maps ↗
16:00–sunset

LATE DAY · YOUR CHOICE

Taragarh Fort or Nawal Sagar views

Choose Taragarh only with enough daylight and comfortable footwear; it is known for overgrown and uneven areas. Otherwise enjoy Nawal Sagar / old-town views and save the fort for a fresh second morning.

SMART PLAN

Keep the fort optional

Taragarh can be rewarding but is not a smooth museum route. If weather, access or energy is poor, choose stepwells and palace art instead; your Bundi trip will still feel complete.

Coming from Kota or by train?

Rajasthan Tourism places Kota about 34 km from Bundi and says Bundi railway station is roughly 4 km south of the old city. Keep arrival day light instead of combining a late check-in with the uphill fort.
