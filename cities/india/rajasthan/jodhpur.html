PLAN A · ONE DAY

The essential Jodhpur route

This is the honest one-day plan: make Mehrangarh the main visit, then descend through the old city to Clock Tower. Do not add Mandore or Osian today.

9–10 hrsBest start: 9:00 AM
Mehrangarh Fort→Jaswant Thada→Old City lunch→Blue City lanes→Clock Tower evening
09:00–12:15

MAIN VISIT · FORT SIDE

Mehrangarh Fort

Start at the fort and give it a proper visit. The museum is officially open daily from 9 AM to 5 PM; allow time for entry, galleries, courtyards and the view.

Ticket rules and audio-guide availability can change—check the official fort website before leaving.

OLD CITY · WALK SLOWLY

Blue City lanes + Toorji Ka Jhalra

Walk through the old lanes with respect: many blue houses are homes, not attractions. Keep Toorji Ka Jhalra as a short heritage stop, then continue toward the market.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

Jodhpur, properly explored

This is the RouteSathi recommendation. Day 1 is the fort and Blue City. Day 2 adds one calm morning outdoors plus the royal museum side—without repeating the same market route.

2 full daysRecommended plan

DAY 1 · Fort, blue lanes & market

  1. 09:00Mehrangarh FortAllow around 3 hours for the museum and fort complex.
  2. 12:30Jaswant ThadaKeep this nearby fort-side heritage stop next.
  3. 13:45Old City lunchEat before the afternoon walking route.
  4. 15:15Blue City lanes + Toorji Ka JhalraWalk respectfully through residential lanes.
  5. 17:15Clock Tower & Sardar MarketShopping, snacks and dinner in one finish.

DAY 2 · Rock park & royal side

  1. 08:00Rao Jodha Desert Rock ParkGo early, especially in warmer months. It is near Mehrangarh but is a nature walk, not a rushed photo stop.
  2. 10:30Breakfast / early meal near the fort sideRest before crossing to the palace side.
  3. 12:00Umaid Bhawan Palace MuseumVisit the public museum area; the palace also includes a hotel and private residence, so do not expect access to every part.
  4. 14:30Lunch around the palace / Ratanada sideStay on the same side for the afternoon.
  5. 17:00One easy eveningUse this for a relaxed café, local dinner or the market only if you still want to return—no need to force another attraction.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Slow city, then one extra day

Keep the first two days exactly as above. On Day 3, choose one extra route—Mandore for local heritage or Osian as a real separate day trip.

3 daysRelaxed Jodhpur
DAY 1

Blue City core

Mehrangarh, Jaswant Thada, old-city lanes, Toorji Ka Jhalra and Clock Tower evening.

DAY 2

Nature + royal museum

Rao Jodha Desert Rock Park in the cooler hours, then Umaid Bhawan Museum and a relaxed meal.

DAY 3

Choose one extra route

Option A: Mandore Gardens and a calm local day. Option B: Osian temples / desert outing with a pre-booked cab—this is a separate day, not an add-on.

Why 2 days is best:It lets you enjoy the fort and old city without racing, then leaves space for the palace museum and one quieter side of Jodhpur.

WHERE TO STAY

Choose your base

Old City / Clock Tower: best for fort views, lanes and market evenings. Ratanada / Umaid Bhawan side: quieter roads and airport convenience. Paota: a practical option for road travel, but plan auto/cab time for sightseeing.
