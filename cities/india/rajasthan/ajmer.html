FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How many days do you have?

Ajmer Sharif is the city’s essential spiritual stop; Adhai Din Ka Jhonpra and Soniji Ki Nasiyan fit naturally into an old-city heritage block. Ana Sagar is best kept for the late afternoon. Pushkar is a short road connection, but deserves its own day.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Old city first.
Lake after heat.

Keep the morning walkable: Ajmer Sharif and Adhai Din Ka Jhonpra. After lunch, choose one cultural stop and finish near Ana Sagar. It prevents the unnecessary zig-zag that makes a short Ajmer visit tiring.
