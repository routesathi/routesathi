AJMER · RAJASTHAN

Ajmer, with respect and a clear route.

A practical city plan for Ajmer Sharif, old-city heritage, Ana Sagar sunset and an optional Pushkar day—without criss-crossing the city.

Pick your number of days Open Ajmer in Maps ↗

START HERE

One day is enough for Ajmer’s core. Two days feel complete.

Keep the Dargah and old-city monuments together in the morning. Leave Ana Sagar for the cooler evening. Add Pushkar only when you have a second day, so neither place feels rushed.

Best baseNear station / civil lines for easy arrivals; old city only if you prefer walking access.
Best timeMorning for shrine and monuments; sunset for Ana Sagar.
Smart routeOld city first, lake later. Pushkar is a separate day trip.

PLAN B · TWO DAYS

Ajmer + Pushkar, properly

Recommended

Day 1 stays inside Ajmer. Day 2 becomes a relaxed Pushkar visit—this is the sensible way to do both.

DAY 1 · AJMER

Ajmer Sharif → Adhai Din Ka Jhonpra → lunch → Soniji Ki Nasiyan / old city → Ana Sagar sunset.

DAY 2 · PUSHKAR

Leave after breakfast for Pushkar. Ghats, Brahma Temple area, market and sunset should be done at a slower pace.

See the Pushkar guide →

Ajmer–Pushkar is roughly a 15–20 km road connection. Traffic, festival days and local movement can change the travel time.

DO NOT MISS THESE

More of Ajmer, when you have time.

SPIRITUAL

Ajmer Sharif Dargah

The city’s key spiritual stop. Visit quietly, dress respectfully and verify current entry rules before leaving.

HERITAGE

Adhai Din Ka Jhonpra

A historic monument near the old-city route; it is easiest to visit with the Dargah area.

EVENING

Ana Sagar & Baradari

Keep the lake side for late afternoon or sunset, not the middle of a hot day.

EXTRA DAY

Pushkar

Do not squeeze it into a rushed Ajmer day. Give it the second day for a better experience.
