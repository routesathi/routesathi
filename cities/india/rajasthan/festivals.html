Plan around the atmosphere

Rajasthan festivals, without the last-minute confusion.

A festival can make a Rajasthan trip unforgettable—but it can also change crowds, stays and local movement. Choose the experience you want, then plan a calm route around it.

Important: Festival dates, processions, venue access and road rules can change every year. The periods below are planning cues, not a promise of exact dates. Before booking, check the latest Rajasthan Tourism and organiser notice.

OCTOBER – NOVEMBER · PUSHKAR

Pushkar Fair

The annual camel and livestock fair brings performances, competitions, pilgrims and a major rush for stays and transport.

How to plan any festival trip

  1. Choose the festival first, then select a stay near your first morning stop.
  2. Book earlier than a normal weekend; popular dates can fill rooms and trains.
  3. Keep one flexible evening and avoid a tight intercity transfer on the event day.
  4. Check official event, temple or venue updates 24–48 hours before leaving.

Want the quieter version?

Visit a few days before or after the main event. You can still enjoy the destination, but with simpler hotel choices, easier movement and more time at the monuments.
