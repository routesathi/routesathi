FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How many days do you have?

Rajasthan Tourism calls Keoladeo one of the world’s important bird breeding and feeding grounds, with over 370 resident and migrant species. Its well-defined trails can be covered on foot, by cycle or by rickshaw; trained rickshaw pullers can be useful bird guides.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Wetland first.
City after birds.

Keoladeo works best when you give it the cool morning and a second quieter window later. Keep palace and fort visits in the city block, so there is no back-and-forth in the hottest hours.

MAIN STOP · WETLAND

Keoladeo National Park entry

Start early, check current entry rules and decide your pace: walk for a short route, cycle for more distance, or choose a park rickshaw. If you want bird identification, ask for an authorised/trained guide or rickshaw guide.

Rajasthan Tourism notes the museum’s antiques, sculptures, local art and manuscripts inside the palace compound. Check same-day access before going.

SMART BIRDING

Choose slow transport

Cycle, walk or rickshaw all work. Rajasthan Tourism says trained rickshaw pullers can be knowledgeable bird guides. Ask the current rate beforehand and never demand close wildlife access.

Check official park details ↗

Apna travel style choose karke Bharatpur plan ka best adjustment dekhein.

Coming by train?

Rajasthan Tourism says Bharatpur is on the Delhi–Mumbai broad-gauge line and the railway station is about 5 km from Keoladeo. Arrive the evening before if you want the best early-morning park start.
