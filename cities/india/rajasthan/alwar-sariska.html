YOUR READY ROUTE

Start in Alwar.
Give the forest time.

Rajasthan Tourism lists Alwar City Palace, Moosi Maharani ki Chhatri, Siliserh Lake and Sariska as key experiences. Confirm opening/access on the day—especially any fort or forest entry.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Heritage, forest and no pressure

Choose this when travel timing matters or you want two safari chances. The extra day is a buffer—not a reason to add random distant stops.

3 easy daysBest slow plan
DAY 1

Alwar city

Museum/City Palace area, Moosi Maharani ki Chhatri, food and a relaxed Siliserh evening.

DAY 2

First confirmed safari

Keep the day around your official Sariska reporting slot and rest properly afterwards.

DAY 3

Second safari or relaxed exit

Use a second confirmed forest drive if available; otherwise leave after a calm breakfast.

Why 3 days works:Wildlife needs flexibility. Two forest chances and a proper arrival make the experience better without a false tiger-sighting promise.
