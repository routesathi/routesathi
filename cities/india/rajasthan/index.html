CHOOSE YOUR TRIP

What kind of Rajasthan trip?

Start simple. You do not need to cover every famous place in one visit.

FIRST RAJASTHAN TRIP

Jaipur + Udaipur

Pick Jaipur for fort-and-market energy, then Udaipur for a slower lake-city finish. Give each city at least two full days.

HERITAGE FOCUS

Jaipur + Jodhpur

For big forts, royal spaces and old-city walks. Do not plan same-day intercity sightseeing after a long road or train journey.

ONE CITY, DONE WELL

Choose your pace

Use Jaipur, Udaipur or Jodhpur alone when time is short. A clear two-day city plan is better than three rushed cities.

SEASONAL NOTE

Plan for the weather,
not just the photos.

October to March is generally the most comfortable period for forts, old-city walks and desert travel. Summer sightseeing needs early starts, sun protection and realistic outdoor time. During monsoon, Udaipur can be especially green, while weather-sensitive plans should always stay flexible.
