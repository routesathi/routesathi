PLAN A · ONE DAY

The essential Udaipur route

This is the honest one-day plan: City Palace and the walkable Old City first, then one Lake Pichola evening. Do not add Fateh Sagar or Monsoon Palace today.

City Palace→Jagdish Temple→Old City lunch→Gangaur Ghat→Lake evening

MAIN VISIT · OLD CITY

City Palace Museum

Reach around opening time and give the palace a proper unhurried visit. City Palace is on Lake Pichola, so it naturally starts the Old City route.

Official visitor hours are published as 9:30 AM–5:30 PM; verify ticket and same-day details before leaving.

OLD CITY · LAKE SIDE

Gangaur Ghat → Bagore ki Haveli

Walk toward Gangaur Ghat and Bagore ki Haveli. The haveli and ghat are together, so this is a natural continuation—not a separate cross-city ride.

EVENING · CHOOSE ONE

Lake Pichola boat / Dharohar show / lakeside dinner

Choose one main evening experience. A Lake Pichola ride gives views of the Lake Palace and Jag Mandir; the Lake Palace itself is not a normal public interior visit. Alternatively choose the Dharohar dance show at Bagore ki Haveli, then dinner nearby.

Boat and show availability, price and timings change—confirm locally before paying.

PLAN C · THREE DAYS

Slow down and enjoy the lake city

Keep the first two days exactly as above. The third day is a choice day—use it for craft, family time or a nearby heritage outing, not for repeating the same Pichola circuit.

3 daysRelaxed Udaipur
DAY 1

Old City core

City Palace, Jagdish Temple, Gangaur Ghat, Bagore ki Haveli and one Lake Pichola evening.

DAY 2

Fateh Sagar side

Saheliyon ki Bari, Moti Magri, Fateh Sagar, then Monsoon Palace only if weather and return timing are comfortable.

DAY 3

Choose your slow side

Option A: Shilpgram plus relaxed shopping. Option B: Doodh Talai Ropeway and a calm local evening. Option C: a pre-planned nearby heritage day with a cab.

Why 2 days is best:It lets you enjoy the palace and lake side on one day, then the gardens and Fateh Sagar side on another—without making every stop feel like a checklist.
