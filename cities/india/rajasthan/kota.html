FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How many days do you have?

Rajasthan Tourism highlights the Garh Palace, Rao Madho Singh Museum, Kishore Sagar, Chambal River Front, Chambal Garden and Garadia Mahadev. One city day and one nature day is the sensible split.

YOUR READY ROUTE

Palace first.
River at sunset.

Kota Garh and the museum work best in the morning. Keep Kishore Sagar and the riverfront for evening, when the lake and lights make more sense.

SMART PLAN

Keep nature separate

Coming from Bundi?

Rajasthan Tourism lists Bundi roughly 35 km from Kota. A short transfer is possible, but Garh Palace plus a riverfront evening works better after an early arrival or an overnight stay.
