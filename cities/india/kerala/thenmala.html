Before you go / निकलने से पहले

Thenmala is an official ecotourism area with different Culture, Leisure and Adventure zones. Book or confirm the exact available activity before building the day: rain, water level, group size, forest restrictions and maintenance can change what operates. Stay on marked paths, use safety gear and never treat a wildlife sighting or a water activity as guaranteed.

FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much eco-tourism time do you actually have?

One day is enough for a confirmed activity, the Leisure/Culture-side public areas and one optional guided walk or boat if actually running. Two days give Palaruvi or a separately booked nature programme its own space. That keeps tickets, weather and safety simple.
