Before you go / निकलने से पहले

Thiruvananthapuram is a capital city with one very important temple rule: Kerala Tourism states that only Hindus may enter Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, with a strict dress code. Everyone can still use the public East Fort and museum-city route respectfully. Museum openings also vary: Napier Museum is closed Mondays and has a later Wednesday start.

RouteSathi guide update: October 2026 · Confirm temple worship slots, dress code, museum tickets and city traffic before travelling.

YOUR READY CAPITAL PLAN

Temple rules first.
Then the city.

Kerala Tourism places Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple, East Fort and the Napier Museum campus among Thiruvananthapuram’s defining heritage stops. This route is deliberately inclusive: it makes the city useful whether or not a visitor is eligible for temple entry.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

East Fort morning, then one museum-campus afternoon

Begin at the public East Fort area. Eligible Hindu visitors can use the current Padmanabhaswamy worship slot only with the required attire; everyone else should keep to public streets and exterior views. After lunch, use Napier Museum and one adjacent cultural block only if it is open.

SREE PADMANABHASWAMY TEMPLE · CURRENT RULES FIRST

Enter only when the published eligibility and dress rules are met

Kerala Tourism states that only Hindus are permitted inside and gives a strict dress code; worship hours can change for festivals. If you are eligible, confirm the live slot and follow staff instructions. If not, continue with the public East Fort route without trying to enter.

PLAN B · TWO FULL DAYS

Capital core on Day 1; one checked city-side extension on Day 2

Day 1 is East Fort plus the Napier campus. Day 2 chooses only one: a confirmed Kuthiramalika visit near East Fort, a longer museum/zoo campus block, or Veli Tourist Village in a different coast-side direction. The best option depends on the day’s actual access and your interests.
