FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much Kollam time do you actually have?

One day is enough for Ashtamudi’s town-side lake edge, a genuine midday break and the Thangassery coast. Two days give space for one additional water-or-nature direction. The long backwater cruise to Alappuzha is not an "evening activity"—treat it as a separately confirmed travel choice.

YOUR READY KOLLAM PLAN

One lake edge.
One coast evening.

Kerala Tourism describes Kollam through Ashtamudi’s backwater network, its coast and the Thangassery area. This route uses one close city-and-lake block, then one coast block. Water transport, lighthouse access and sea conditions are day-of decisions, not guarantees.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Ashtamudi in the morning, Thangassery for the late light

Use this for a first visit. Start by the public lake edge while the day is cooler. If a licensed, safe and currently operating short water option is available, choose it; otherwise enjoy the shore and keep the route on land. Save the heritage coast for after a proper rest.

ASHTAMUDI LAKE + ASHRAMAM SIDE

Start at the lake, without forcing a boat

Ashtamudi is Kollam’s defining backwater landscape. Begin at a public lake-side area and let current weather decide the pace. This is enough as a meaningful first block even when a cruise, ferry or boat is unavailable.

THANGASSERY HERITAGE SIDE

Use the fort-and-lighthouse area as one block

Kerala Tourism places Thangassery about five kilometres from Kollam town and highlights its lighthouse, fort remnants and breakwater. Walk only through open public areas and confirm current lighthouse entry locally before relying on an inside visit.

THANGASSERY COAST · CONDITIONS FIRST

End at the coast, not in the sea

Use a public promenade or beach edge only if weather, sea conditions and barriers allow it. Stay clear of rough water, breakwater edges and restricted sections; a safe daylight coast pause is better than forcing a sunset photo.

PLAN B · TWO FULL DAYS

Core Kollam on Day 1; one water-or-nature direction on Day 2

Day 1 is the lake-and-coast route above. Day 2 chooses one direction only: Munroe Island for a separately arranged slow canal day, or Sasthamkotta Lake for a different inland lake pause. Do not try to pair both with the core route.
