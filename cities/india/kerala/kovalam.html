Before you go / निकलने से पहले

Kovalam is a three-beach coast, not a guaranteed swimming, surf or watersports plan. Check current sea state, flags, lifeguards, rain and operator conditions locally. Use only designated public areas, never cross barriers or go into rough water, and treat lighthouse access as a same-day confirmation.

FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much coast time do you actually have?

One day is enough for Lighthouse Beach, Hawa Beach and Samudra Beach at an unhurried pace. Two days allow one cultural or quieter coast-side addition. Any water activity must be confirmed on the day with a suitable operator and sea conditions.

YOUR READY COAST PLAN

Follow the coves.
Respect the sea.

Kerala Tourism identifies Kovalam’s three crescent-shaped beaches—Lighthouse, Hawa/Eve’s and Samudra—as its core coast. This plan joins the close public coast together and leaves every water-dependent activity conditional on the actual day.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Lighthouse, Hawa and Samudra at one calm coast pace

Use this for a first visit. Begin near Lighthouse Beach while access is clear, walk the connected public coast, take a real midday break, then end at the quieter Samudra side. Do not use water activities to fill a timetable.

One coast coreBest short route

LIGHTHOUSE BEACH + VIZHINJAM LIGHTHOUSE

Start with the signature coastline

Use the public beach and promenade first. Kerala Tourism highlights Vizhinjam Lighthouse as Kovalam’s prominent coastal viewpoint; climb it only if it is currently open, weather permits and the on-site rules allow it. The beach view itself is enough when access changes.

HAWA / EVE’S BEACH

Walk the middle cove without promising a swim

Hawa Beach is one of Kovalam’s recognised crescent beaches. Use a public beach walk and assess the sea locally. If flags, surf, rain or staff advice say no, enjoy the shore from land and do not treat entering the water as part of the route.

KOVALAM BEACH SIDE

Take a true meal and shade break

Eat, rehydrate and reset during the warmest stretch. A beach route is better with real rest than with a forced far-away attraction in the middle of the day.

SAMUDRA BEACH

Use the quieter cove as the afternoon coast stop

Samudra Beach sits north of the busier coves and is known for a calmer, more separated setting. Use the public shore or sea-wall view, keeping a safe distance from waves and rocks.

RETURN TO LIGHTHOUSE SIDE OR STAY

Use one safe, public sunset view

Choose the closest safe open coast path or your stay’s beach access for evening. Do not cross rocks, closed paths or barriers just to get a different sunset angle; clouds and sea conditions decide the view.

PLAN B · TWO FULL DAYS

Beach core on Day 1; one Vizhinjam-side culture stop on Day 2

Day 1 stays exactly on the Lighthouse–Hawa–Samudra coast route above. On Day 2, choose the nearby Vizhinjam Rock Cut Cave Temple or Kerala Arts and Crafts Village, then stop rather than combining both with a long city sightseeing list.
