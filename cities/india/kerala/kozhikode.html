Before you go / निकलने से पहले

Kozhikode works best as a compact city-and-coast visit, not as a checklist stretching across the whole district. Check current beach conditions, rain, public access, road time and any attraction-specific rules on the day. Use public coastal paths only; sea, barriers, flags and local staff decide whether water-side access is safe.

YOUR READY MALABAR PLAN

Follow the city lanes.
Keep the coast calm.

Official Kozhikode sources highlight Mananchira Square, SM Street, Kozhikode Beach, Beypore, Kappad and Thusharagiri as different experiences. This route groups the close city core first, then uses one separate coast or nature direction only when you actually have the time.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Old city morning, food break, Kozhikode Beach evening

Use this for a first visit. The route has short city transfers and a true midday pause, so the evening beach feels like a finish rather than another item to complete.

MANANCHIRA SQUARE

Start with the city’s historic public heart

Mananchira was once associated with the Zamorin palace grounds and is now a public square with the pond and landscaped space. Walk it before the city gets busy, while respecting any local access notices or maintenance work.

SM STREET / MITTAI THERUVU

Take the heritage food street slowly

SM Street is Kozhikode’s well-known sweet and shopping lane. Browse the public street, try a snack from a place that suits you, and keep walking easy—there is no reason to mix it with a distant beach before lunch.

CITY LUNCH + REAL REST

Give Malabar food and the afternoon proper space

Use a genuine meal and rest near the city centre or your stay. Kozhikode is known for its Malabar food traditions; this buffer keeps the beach evening comfortable rather than heat-heavy.

KOZHIKODE BEACH PROMENADE

Walk the public coast before sunset

The city beach is best used for its promenade, breeze and local evening rhythm. Stay in open public areas, follow barriers and do not assume sea entry or pier-side access is safe on a given day.

End at the coast, not in traffic

PLAN B · TWO FULL DAYS

City core on Day 1; Beypore port coast on Day 2

Day 1 is the city route above. On Day 2, use Beypore as a separate south-side port-and-coast block. Its beach, pulimuttu walkway and maritime character deserve unhurried time; any workshop visit needs its own confirmed responsible access.
