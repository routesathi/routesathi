Before you go / निकलने से पहले

Munnar is hill-road travel, not a flat-city checklist. Mist, rain, traffic, park closures and viewpoint visibility can change quickly. Start early, keep one corridor per day, carry a light layer and confirm Eravikulam entry/booking before committing your plan.

RouteSathi guide update: October 2026 · Confirm current park access, weather and road conditions before leaving.

FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How many hill-road days do you really have?

One day can only give you a focused Munnar taste. Two days are the balanced plan: town/park one day, east-side lake-and-viewpoint corridor the next. A third day is only for a confirmed trek or one separate valley.

YOUR READY HILL PLAN

Less driving.
More Munnar.

Kerala Tourism places Munnar’s tea landscape, Eravikulam National Park, Mattupetty and Top Station among its key experiences. This plan gives each direction real time rather than treating every famous name as a roadside five-minute stop.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Central Munnar and Eravikulam—only if entry is confirmed

Use a park morning only with a valid current booking/access confirmation. If the park is closed, switch to the Tea Museum and a slow tea-landscape day instead of trying to force a distant Top Station dash.

ERAVIKULAM NATIONAL PARK / RAJAMALA

Use the morning only with confirmed current entry

Eravikulam has controlled access and can close or change operation for conservation, weather or other reasons. Check the official/current booking information before leaving your stay; the route works even if you choose the tea-town backup.

PLAN B · TWO FULL DAYS

Park/town on Day 1; Mattupetty to Top Station on Day 2

Day 1 is the central Munnar route above. On Day 2, travel outward through the eastern corridor in order: Mattupetty, Echo Point, Kundala and Top Station. Return once, with weather and traffic buffer.
