Before you go / निकलने से पहले

Bekal is a historic coastal fort, not a guaranteed swimming or late-night beach plan. Check current fort opening, ticketing, weather and beach safety on the day. Use marked public paths inside the fort, respect barriers and do not climb walls, enter tunnels or go close to rough water.

FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much north-Kerala time do you actually have?

One day is enough for Bekal Fort and the close coast. Two days give Kappil Beach or Chandragiri Fort proper time. Ananthapura Lake Temple is another distinct Kasaragod-side trip and should not be forced into a beach sunset schedule.

YOUR READY NORTH-KERALA PLAN

Follow the ramparts.
Keep the coast calm.

Kerala Tourism describes Bekal Fort as a 17th-century sea-facing fort, with the beach and public coast close by. This route makes the fort the core visit, then leaves all beach time and longer Kasaragod detours dependent on current conditions and real travel time.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Bekal Fort in the morning, coast in the afternoon

Use this for a first visit. Start at the fort when the day is cooler, take a proper lunch, then use the nearby beach or public walkway for a relaxed afternoon. There is no reason to add distant Kasaragod sights to this core day.

PLAN B · TWO FULL DAYS

Bekal core on Day 1; choose Kappil or Chandragiri on Day 2

Day 1 is the fort-and-coast route above. On Day 2 choose one direction only: Kappil Beach for a quieter coastal block, or Chandragiri Fort for a separate river-and-sea heritage view. They do not need to share a rushed day.
