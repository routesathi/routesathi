How much real time do you have in Thekkady?

YOUR READY FOREST PLAN

Book the slot.
Then build the day.

Periyar Tiger Reserve’s official boating programme runs on Periyar Lake and requires travellers to report at Anavachal Parking before taking the reserve bus to the boat landing. This guide puts that real reporting chain first, then leaves unbooked time calm and close by.

PLAN A · ONE FULL DAY

Official early boat slot and a slow Kumily afternoon

Use this plan only when you hold the correct official boating ticket. The official programme lists a 07:30 departure with a 06:30 reporting time at Anavachal Parking; always follow the slot and reporting time printed on your live booking.

One forest blockBest short route
Anavachal report→Reserve bus→Periyar Lake boat→Kumily afternoon
06:30–07:00 · TICKET + ID CHECK

ANAVACHAL PARKING / PTR REPORTING

Report for the exact boat slot you booked

Carry the original ID used during booking and arrive at the official reporting location stated by Periyar Tiger Reserve. The reserve lists early reporting because the boat landing is separate from the parking area; do not assume a late hotel pickup will work.

07:00–07:30 · RESERVE TRANSFER

PTR BUS TO BOAT LANDING

Let the official reserve transfer set the pace

Periyar Tiger Reserve says the boat landing is about 4 km from Anavachal Parking and visitors use reserve buses. Follow staff instructions and reach the landing before the time required for your booked boat.

Open Periyar Boat Landing in Maps ↗
07:30–09:00 · 90 MINUTES

PERIYAR LAKE BOATING

Use the boat for landscape, birds and chance sightings

The official boat trip is about 1.5 hours. Wear the life jacket, remain seated and follow the crew. Elephants, deer, gaur, birds or otters may appear around the lake, but the forest decides the sightings—not the booking.
