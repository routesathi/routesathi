Before you go / निकलने से पहले

Guruvayur is a living place of worship, not a quick sightseeing stop. Check current darshan availability, entry policies, dress requirements, festival notices and phone/camera rules directly with Guruvayur Devaswom before leaving. The temple’s official site lists traditional dress requirements; on-site staff and temple instructions are final.
