FIRST, CHOOSE YOUR PACE

How much North-Kerala time do you actually have?

One day is enough for a focused Kannur town, fort and coast route. Two days is the balanced first trip: Day 1 around Kannur, Day 2 on the Thalassery–Muzhappilangad side. A ritual night, Dharmadam or an inland temple is an extra—not something to squeeze between beaches.

Fort mornings.

Kerala Tourism identifies Kannur through its Malabar forts, coastal landscapes and Theyyam traditions. This route keeps the city-side heritage day separate from Thalassery’s fort-and-drive-in-beach corridor. Sea conditions, monument entry and ritual schedules are always live decisions.

St. Angelo in the morning, Payyambalam at safe daylight

Use this when Kannur is your base or you only have one full day. Stay close to town: fort first, one optional heritage stop, then a proper rest and a public coast evening. Do not add Thalassery or Muzhappilangad to this day.
