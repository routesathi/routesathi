TAMIL NADU · SEASONAL GUIDE

Choose the season,
then the route.

Tamil Nadu can mean hot city days, rain-sensitive coast conditions, temple festivals or cooler hill travel. Start with a RouteSathi guide, then use current weather, event and access information before setting out.

RouteSathi rule: A good Chennai plan protects the early morning, a real midday break and one coast evening. Weather, temple customs, public access and sea conditions matter more than a fixed checklist.
NOVEMBER – FEBRUARY

More comfortable city walks and coast evenings

This is generally the easier period for Chennai’s outdoor heritage walks and a Marina or Besant Nagar evening. It can also be busier around holiday/festival periods, so leave room for traffic and check individual site schedules.

Chennai: Mylapore + MarinaUse the earlier part of the day for Mylapore and San Thome, then keep the coast for a calmer finish.Chennai: museum + Fort dayCheck live entry hours and holiday closures before using the central heritage route.

Plan smart: Comfortable weather does not remove crowd, traffic or current sea-safety rules. Keep the same practical route structure.

MARCH – JUNE

Early starts and protected afternoons

The city can be hot and humid. Use the temple/heritage exterior in the early morning, take a long shaded or indoor afternoon break, then choose a sea-facing evening only when current conditions are comfortable.

Chennai one-day routeBegin Mylapore early; retain the lunch/rest block instead of adding an extra landmark in peak heat.Live Metro plannerUse the official planner for a current route and do not estimate travel only by map distance.

Plan smart: Carry water, use sun protection and accept a shorter route when heat makes an outdoor stop uncomfortable.

OCTOBER – DECEMBER · WEATHER CHECK

Rain can change the day faster than a saved plan

Northeast-monsoon weather can affect roads, beach conditions and the comfort of long outdoor walks. Select indoor heritage or food time as a backup, and follow local weather, transport and site instructions on the day.

Indoor heritage backupUse the Government Museum only after checking its live opening day and access details.Marina safety checkThe promenade may still be an option, but sea conditions and official advice decide the actual plan.

Plan smart: Do not plan swimming at Marina. Chennai District warns of strong undercurrents; signs and on-site safety staff are final.

Before you book

Weather, temple arrangements, museum/fort hours, transport and coast access can change. Tamil Nadu Tourism, Chennai District, the official Metro planner and on-site instructions are the final authority before you travel.
