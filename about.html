ABOUT ROUTESATHI

India travel, planned
without confusion.

RouteSathi is an independent travel-planning guide for people who want a practical, well-paced India trip—not just a long list of places.

Travel better when
the route makes sense.

A good trip is not about trying to cover every famous place. It is about choosing the right area, keeping realistic travel time, knowing what needs advance checking and leaving room to enjoy the day.

RouteSathi turns that thinking into simple city guides: day-wise plans, sensible map routes, stay and food guidance, seasonal notes and important checks before leaving.

OUR PROMISE

We will keep guides practical, honest and easy to use. If a plan is too rushed, we will say so. If timings or access can change, we will ask you to verify before leaving.

HOW WE HELP
